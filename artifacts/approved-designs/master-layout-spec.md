# VYVA Master Layout Lock

Approved visual references:

- `home-main-approved.png`
- `category-level-template-approved.png`
- `health-plan-approved.png` clean capture, no watermark.

The category-level page pattern is:

- Header with VYVA mark, time/date, and profile.
- One large warm hero card with a soft background image and a single left-aligned main message.
- One quiet icon-only mic affordance on the right side of the hero, with no decorative chat icon or visible `Talk to VYVA` text.
- Four large primary cards in a 2 by 2 grid.
- One white `Fast help` panel with three visible rows.
- Bottom navigation remains `Home / SOS / My Reports`.

Approved category copy:

- My Mind: `Strengthen Memory`, `Train Reflexes`, `Improve Thinking`, `Sharpen Senses`.
- My Health category: `Feel Better`, `My Medication`, `My Vitals`, `Age Well`.
- My Community: `Make Friends`, `Join In`, `Share Stories`, `What's On`.
- My Concierge: `Home Care`, `Personal Care`, `Order In`, `Book Now`.
- My Medication: `My Reminders`, `My Refills`, `Stay Safe`, `Home Remedies`.

Approved Health Plan copy:

- Hero: `Health Plan Ready`.
- Cards: `Medicine`, `Vitals`, `Symptoms`, `Prevention`.
- Fast help starts with useful safety/plan/report rows.

Session rule:

- When VYVA is already active, hide the hero voice CTA and let the minimized dock open VYVA.
