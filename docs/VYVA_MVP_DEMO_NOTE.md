# VYVA MVP Demo Note

## Demo Path

Start at `/vyva-demo`.

## Demo Users

- Maria Lopez, senior
- John Miller, senior
- Ana Lopez, caregiver

## Maria Demonstrates

Maria shows the steady-path story. Her recent check-ins, routines, and small-step guidance show a mostly steady wellbeing trend. Use her flow to show the senior home, weekly Brain & Wellbeing check-in, My Week insight cards, and the Ask for Help button.

## John Demonstrates

John shows an attention-recommended story. His demo data includes a quieter week, routine friction, and reminder logging changes. Use his flow to show that VYVA can surface a change from usual pattern without showing raw scores or scary labels.

## Ana Demonstrates

Ana shows the caregiver view. She can see both assigned seniors, review alerts, add notes, and compare high-level wellbeing statuses. John has detailed sharing turned off, so Ana sees consent messaging instead of private check-in answers, detailed evidence, medication reminder details, or routine detail.

## Live Demo Script

1. Open `/vyva-demo`.
2. Continue as Maria and show the steady senior home.
3. Start the weekly Brain & Wellbeing check-in, answer the questions, and open My Week.
4. Return to Maria home and press Ask for Help to show the minimal caregiver alert behavior.
5. Return to `/vyva-demo` and continue as Ana.
6. Open Maria to show the steady pattern.
7. Open John to show attention recommended, consent-off privacy, alert review, and note entry.

## Intentionally Excluded

- No raw score display for seniors.
- No treatment planning or medication-change guidance.
- No medical assessment labels or future illness claims.
- No external APIs in the demo flow.
- No replacement for emergency services, a doctor, or a pharmacist.

## Safety And Privacy Limits

VYVA is positioned as a wellbeing support demo. It can show trends, changes from usual pattern, small prevention steps, reminders, logging, caregiver notes, and caregiver alerts. Sharing consent controls whether a caregiver can see private check-in answers and detailed evidence. Pressing Ask for Help may still create a minimal help alert so the senior can request follow-up even when detailed sharing is off.
